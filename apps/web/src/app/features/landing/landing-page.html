What comes back

One screenshot in, one structured report out — the same parts every time, including the part that says the chart supports nothing.

01

Structure and regime

Where the chart is in its swing structure, how volatile it is against its own recent range, and whether moves are following through.

02

Level zones

The zones price has actually reacted in — as bands, with the touch count behind each one. Orders cluster around a level rather than at it, and a single tidy number would claim a precision the chart does not contain.

03

A setup, or an honest no

One conditional scenario — trigger zone, invalidation, target, horizon — plus the observable event that would prove the read wrong. When the chart supports nothing, it says so instead of inventing a trade.
