The read, taken apart

One read, field by field, and what you do with each one.

One output for RELIANCE on the 1 hour chart, read at 14:32 IST. Every number comes with the condition that retires it.

RELIANCE NSE · 1H · candlestick 1 credit · 14:32 IST
Trend state
Transition clarity 3 of 5

The old trend has stopped and the next one has not started. Clarity 3 means size down, not sit out.

Volatility regime
Expanding ATR(14) ₹18.20 · 1.23% of price

Ranges are widening, so yesterday's stop distance is now too tight.

Resistance zone
₹1,504.00 – 1,512.60
4 touches · 1.8 ATR above price

A band, not a line, because price reacted across it four times. 1.8 ATR away is about two normal candles.

Support zone
₹1,441.20 – 1,449.80
3 touches · 1.6 ATR below price

Three touches is a working zone, not a proven floor.

A transition has two live paths, so both are stated. You pick the one you want.

Falsifier

A 1H close above ₹1,512.60 on volume below the 20 period average. That reads as the zone being tested rather than accepted, and scenario A stops applying.

Read this field first. It is the line that tells you when to stop believing the rest.

Base rate from analogous setups

29 of 61 comparable transitions on this symbol and timeframe since 2019 reached the upper target before the invalidation level.

The count is shown so you can see how thin the sample is. It is a hint, not a backtest.

Why it is worth trusting

There is no accuracy score here, and that is deliberate.

One headline number tells you nothing about the chart in front of you. Three things do the work instead.

It commits to a falsifier

Every read names the condition that would break it, written before the outcome is known. You can check it against the chart yourself.

It shows the base rate and the count

How often similar setups resolved, out of how many. A thin sample looks thin instead of looking confident.

It declines charts it cannot read

A cropped screenshot or too few candles returns a refusal, not a guess. The credit comes back automatically.
